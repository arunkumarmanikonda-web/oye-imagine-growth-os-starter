# H3 Super-Admin Control Center

Generated at UTC: 2026-08-08T07:43:01Z

## Scope
H3 formalizes the super-admin control plane for:
- tenant governance
- feature flag and entitlement oversight
- AI routing controls
- health and finance inspection
- support impersonation with explicit audit expectations

## Core operating expectations
- super-admin actions must be traceable and intentional
- tenant-level overrides must remain visible and reviewable
- platform health and finance signals must be aggregated at the tenant layer
- impersonation must require a support ticket and a durable reason
